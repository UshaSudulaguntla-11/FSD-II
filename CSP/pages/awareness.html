💡 Water Awareness

Learn simple ways to save water, protect water sources, and maintain a healthy village.

🚰

Fix Water Leaks

Report leaking taps and damaged pipelines immediately to avoid unnecessary water wastage.

🚿

Use Water Wisely

Turn off taps when they are not being used and avoid wasting clean water.

🌧️

Save Rainwater

Promote rainwater harvesting to improve water availability during dry seasons.

🧼

Maintain Hygiene

Use safe and clean water for drinking, cooking, and household activities.

🗑️

Protect Water Sources

Do not throw garbage or chemicals into lakes, ponds, wells, or other water sources.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Spread Awareness

Encourage family members and neighbours to follow safe water and conservation practices.



💧 WaterWatch Tip


Every drop counts. Small changes in daily water usage can make a big difference to the entire community.
